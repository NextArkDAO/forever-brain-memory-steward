# Synthetic Fixtures

Competition-safe fictional companion data used for development, adversarial verification, and the public demonstration.

Fixtures must be visibly labeled synthetic and must not be transformed copies of private or personal material.

`aster-continuity.json` is the public demonstration fixture. Aster and Morgan are fictional and do not represent a real person or companion.

`language-context-cases.json` tests paraphrases, temporary exceptions, sarcasm, slang, punctuation, emoji, correction, and uncertainty. Its examples are original and fictional. Expected results constrain memory behavior; they do not label a real person's inner state.
